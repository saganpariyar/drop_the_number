## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Drop The Number does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**

### Local Device Storage
The game saves game state and statistics locally on your device using Android `SharedPreferences`:
- Current High Score
- Highest Merged Tile (e.g. 2048, 4096)
- Sound and Haptic Feedback Preferences (Muted / Enabled)

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any external server or cloud service. Uninstalling the app clears this local data.

---

## 2. Permissions Used
Drop The Number requires minimal device permissions:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when dropping or merging number blocks.

The app does **not** request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, device identifiers, or network state.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The core game runs 100% offline. We do not integrate any third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioral advertising SDKs that harvest user data.
